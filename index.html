Bull ants (Paraponera clavata)

Information about the Bull ant:

Paraponera clavata, commonly known as the bullet ant, is a species of ant known for its extremely painful sting. It inhabits humid lowland rainforests in Central and South America.
Bullet ants are one of the largest ant species in the world, it’s also got one of the longest foraging ranges, and is fiercely territorial.
Aside from its extraordinary size (see below), the bullet ant’s most striking features are its large mandibles, hairy thorax and long stinger. While it has a classic ant silhouette – six legs and a segmented body – its stinger gives it the look of a wingless wasp. Its scientific name, clavata, means ‘club-shaped’ and refers to the shape of the insect’s head.
